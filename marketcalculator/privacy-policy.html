Privacy Policy for Market Calculator

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Market Calculator is a shopping calculator operated by Logan CHOI. It lets you enter items, quantities, prices, discounts and optional notes and retain shopping records on your device.

Information stored on your device

Shopping records and draft entries are stored locally. The app does not require an account. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the local records.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on applicable settings and consent, these services may collect or share information such as IP address, device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google describes its processing in its Privacy Policy. Privacy choices, where required, are available in the app's settings.
