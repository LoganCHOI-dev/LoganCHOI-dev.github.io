Privacy Policy — Finger Chooser

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Finger Chooser ("the app") is a simple tool that randomly picks a finger, splits people into teams, or sets an order by touching the screen. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Finger Chooser does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. The app has no account, no login, and no server owned by the developer. Your settings (such as the chosen mode, sound and vibration preferences, selected language, and ad-frequency counters) are stored only on your device and never leave it.

The core feature — randomly choosing fingers — works fully offline and needs no internet connection.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, part of Google Mobile Ads. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process certain information, such as a device advertising identifier, coarse information needed to deliver and measure ads, and consent choices. This processing is carried out by Google, not by the developer. The app itself does not send your settings or any content you create to advertising systems.

In regions where it is required, you will be shown a consent form before personalized ads are used, and you can change your choice later from the app's Settings under "Privacy & consent options" when available.

For details on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

The app requests internet access only so that the advertising component can load ads when a connection is available. The random-choice features do not require any permission and continue to work without a network.

Data storage and deletion

All app data is kept locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Finger Chooser is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
