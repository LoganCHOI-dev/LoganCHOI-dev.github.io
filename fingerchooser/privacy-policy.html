Privacy Policy — Finger Chooser

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Logan CHOI provides Finger Chooser for Android. The app lets a group pick winners, make teams, set a playing order and play three touch games on one device.

Information we collect

You can use Finger Chooser without an account. The app saves preferences such as sound, vibration and language on your device. We use Google's advertising and analytics services as described below.

The core feature — randomly choosing fingers — works fully offline and needs no internet connection.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share an IP address (used to estimate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) processes consent choices.

When the app opens, it checks with UMP to see whether a consent form is required. If UMP provides a required form, the app displays it. When available, you can revisit your choices in Settings under "Privacy & consent options". The app requests ads only when UMP indicates that it can do so.

For details on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for advertising, consent management and analytics. Games and random selection also work offline.

Usage and advertising analytics

When analytics collection is allowed, Google Analytics for Firebase uses an app instance identifier and collects device and app information, sessions and interactions. We measure when a round starts and finishes, which game was played, whether it began from Home or Play Again, and whether an interstitial was shown or unavailable after a result. These events do not include finger positions or the identity of a winner. We use reports to understand which games people play and how ads perform. Linking AdMob and Analytics allows Google to associate ad impressions and estimated revenue with app usage.

Data storage and deletion

You can delete your local preferences by clearing app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Advertising and analytics records already sent to Google are subject to the relevant service's retention settings and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
