Privacy Policy for BrushJoy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

BrushJoy (package name: com.loganchoi.brushteeth) is a toothbrushing timer and camera-play app for young children and their caregivers. This policy explains the information used by the App, information stored on your device, and processing by its advertising provider.

1. Developer and scope

The developer does not operate an account service or a server that receives your profiles, photos, brushing records, or camera results. The App does not require registration, an account, or sign-in and does not offer in-app purchases.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising involves processing by Google as explained in Section 5. The App does not include Firebase Analytics or User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent forms and does not send brushing, sticker, or ticket events to Firebase.

If you choose to email the developer, your email address and the information you include in your message are used to respond to that request. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal or security obligations. You can ask for deletion by contacting the same email address. Please do not send a child's photos or sensitive information when requesting support.

2. Camera permission and on-device effects

The App requests camera permission to display the camera view, place playful foam around the mouth, and let a caregiver optionally take a profile photo. A caregiver can grant or withdraw permission in Android settings.

  • Brushing camera frames are processed temporarily on the device using bundled MediaPipe Face Landmarker and toothbrush-detection models. Images, facial or lip positions, and detection results from the brushing effect are not recorded or uploaded by the App.
  • Temporary observations are used to position visual effects and provide play feedback. The App does not identify a person or perform facial identity recognition.
  • Camera permission is required to begin a new camera-based brushing session. Existing sticker books, settings, and profile management can be accessed without starting a camera session.
  • The App does not record video or microphone audio. Music and sound effects are bundled with the App.

3. Optional profile photos and other local records

A caregiver may choose an optional nickname, an app character, or a photo taken expressly for a local profile. A legal name, birth date, email address, or other contact information is not required. We recommend using a nickname that does not identify the child.

Only the photo confirmed by the caregiver is saved as a resized image in the App's private internal storage for that profile. Taking or replacing a profile photo is separate from the temporary camera processing during brushing. The App does not upload profile photos, add them to the shared photo gallery, or provide cloud photo storage.

Temporary capture and crop drafts are stored in the App's private cache while editing. Cancelled drafts are scheduled for deletion; inactive drafts and unreferenced images may be cleaned up after seven days when the App can safely perform cleanup. Only a successfully saved profile adopts the selected image.

The App stores the following on the device to provide its features:

  • Profile nicknames, randomly generated local profile IDs, character choices, and optional profile photos.
  • Per-profile brushing settings, completion counts, sticker books, sticker placement and album dates, and unfinished sticker rewards.
  • Album cover designs, decoration choices and positions, and temporary editing drafts.
  • The device's shared sticker-ticket balance and local records used to prevent duplicate rewards and maintain the advertising/reward flow.
  • Language, music, sound-effect, and other preferences, plus local recovery and migration files used to preserve records when the App is updated.

These local profile and play records are not sent to the developer, Google advertising, Firebase, or a separate analytics service. They are not linked by the App to advertising identifiers. The App does not provide cloud synchronization, export or sharing of profile photos, or sharing of sticker-book records. The optional share-app action sends the App description and store link to Android's share chooser, without a child's profile or camera image.

4. Retention, deletion, and controls

Local records remain in the App until you change or delete them, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. The App excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device backup transfer. Recovery files used inside the App are local files, not cloud backups.

A caregiver can delete a profile from profile management after confirmation. This removes that profile's nickname, photo, brushing records, sticker books, cover designs, and related unfinished rewards and drafts. Other profiles and the shared ticket balance remain. The App records deletion and retries cleanup when a file or earlier recovery copy cannot be removed immediately. A cleanup failure can require another attempt; the App does not promise that every file disappears instantly after a failed operation.

To remove all locally stored profiles and shared records, use Android settings to clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. This cannot be used to recover records later. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely delete your local photos or records because they are not received by the developer.

Google's retention and deletion practices for information processed by its advertising services are governed by Google's policies. Deleting a local profile is not a request to delete Google's advertising-service data. See the links in Section 5 or contact the developer with questions about the App's handling of information.

5. Advertising and third-party processing

The App uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for a persistent bottom banner, an interstitial after the existing completed-session sticker placement, and rewarded ads explicitly selected to receive sticker tickets. Full-screen ads temporarily replace the banner. Advertising is not a condition for starting or completing the core brushing timer.

All ad requests use child-directed treatment, a maximum content rating of G, and the SDK's limited-ads signal before initialization. The App does not request personalized advertising. It removes Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions from its manifest. It does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, or shared-photo-library permission for advertising.

Limited ads do not mean that no information leaves the device. Google may process information needed for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Google's Mobile Ads disclosure describes IP addresses and general location inferred from them, app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or other identifiers where applicable. The App's child-directed and limited-ads settings restrict advertising uses and features; the App blocks Android Advertising ID access. Limited ads can still use IP addresses for delivery, cache ad content, and use fraud-prevention storage where Google's programmatic limited-ads service applies.

The App does not send profile nicknames, profile photos, brushing images, face/lip positions, sticker-book contents, or ticket histories to the advertising SDK. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google determines retention for its own processing; the App does not operate a server-side deletion service for that data.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, child-directed ad settings, and limited ads.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children aged 8 and under with caregiver involvement. A caregiver should manage permissions, optional nicknames and photos, profile deletion, and external links. The App does not ask a child for account registration, a birth date, contact details, or a legal name. The App does not upload children's profile or camera information to the developer.

The child-directed advertising settings and restricted permissions described above apply to all users of this App. If you have questions about children's information or believe it has been sent to the developer, contact us using Section 9.

7. Health notice

BrushJoy supports a daily toothbrushing routine through a timer, general visual guidance, and play rewards. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a medical condition. Camera effects and brushing records do not measure tooth cleanliness, actual brushing coverage, brushing accuracy, or treatment effectiveness. Seek advice from a qualified dental or healthcare professional for oral-health concerns, medical decisions, or treatment.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's features or information-handling practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. Material changes may also be explained within the App or its store listing.
