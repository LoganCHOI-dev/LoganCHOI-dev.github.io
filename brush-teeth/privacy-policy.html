BrushJoy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

BrushJoy is a toothbrushing timer and camera-play app for children aged 8 and under and their caregivers. No account or registration is required.

1. Camera

BrushJoy uses the camera for brushing effects and optional profile photos. Images and detection results used during brushing are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded. The app does not identify individuals through facial recognition.

2. Information stored on your device

Profile nicknames, optional profile photos, brushing records, sticker books, ticket balances, and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage to provide its features. Temporary images used when taking or editing a profile photo also remain on the device.

These records are not uploaded to the developer or shared with advertising providers. The app does not provide cloud synchronization and disables Android backup of its data.

3. Advertising

BrushJoy uses Google AdMob. All ad requests use child-directed and limited-ad settings. The app does not request personalized advertising or access the Android Advertising ID.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device-related identifiers permitted by these settings. This processing supports advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Google may use device storage for ad delivery and fraud prevention. Advertising data is encrypted in transit. Google AdMob data disclosure, Limited ads

Profile information, photos, brushing camera images, and sticker records are not provided to AdMob. Google’s processing, retention, and deletion practices are described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Caregivers can delete a profile and its associated photos and records within the app. Other profiles and the shared ticket balance remain.

To remove all local data, clear BrushJoy’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Camera permission can also be withdrawn in Android settings. The developer cannot access, restore, or remotely delete records stored only on your device.
