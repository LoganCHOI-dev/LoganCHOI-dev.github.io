Privacy Policy for Bogle Bogle Brushing

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Bogle Bogle Brushing (Korean name: 보글보글 양치, referred to below as the “App”) is a brushing timer designed to help caregivers and young children build an enjoyable toothbrushing routine. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when the App is used.

1. Information Collected Directly by the Developer

The developer does not operate a separate server and does not directly collect a user’s name, email address, contact information, camera images, or face detection results. The App does not require an account or login and does not offer in-app purchases.

The App includes advertising through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Information processed by third-party services is described in Section 6.

2. Camera Use

With the user’s permission, the App uses the front-facing camera to detect the area around the mouth and teeth in real time on the device and display a playful foam effect.

  • Camera frames are processed in real time on the device.
  • The App does not take or save photos or videos.
  • Camera frames and face detection results are not sent outside the device.
  • The brushing timer can still be used if camera permission is denied.
  • Camera permission can be changed at any time in Android settings.

3. Information Stored on the Device

The App stores the following information only on the user’s device:

  • Selected brushing duration and sound setting
  • The number of brushing sessions completed today and in total
  • The number of stickers earned and the most recent reward information

This information is used to provide the App’s features and is not intended to identify an individual. It is removed through Android’s standard app-data deletion process when the App is uninstalled. The App is configured to exclude this information from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for children and their caregivers. It does not ask children to enter their name, date of birth, contact details, or other personal information.

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment, and the maximum ad content rating is limited to G. The App removes the Android Advertising ID permission and Privacy Sandbox advertising ID, attribution, and topics permissions from its merged Android manifest.

However, Google advertising services may process information such as approximate location inferred from an IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers such as an App Set ID. Young children should use the App with a caregiver. A caregiver decides whether to grant camera permission and can withdraw it at any time through the device settings.

5. Health Notice

The App is a playful timer that supports brushing habits. The foam shown on screen is a visual effect that reacts to the detected position and movement around the mouth and teeth. It does not assess tooth cleanliness or brushing accuracy, diagnose any condition, or determine whether treatment is effective. Consult a qualified dental professional about any dental or oral-health concern.

6. Third-Party Processing

Google Mobile Ads and UMP

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show a banner ad at the bottom of the home screen. Ads are not shown on brushing preparation, active brushing, completion reward, or sticker album screens.

In regions where privacy choices are required, the App uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present the required choices and, when applicable, provide a way to reopen those choices.

According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, the SDK may automatically collect and share the following information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • App and SDK performance and diagnostic information
  • Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers

The App removes permissions related to the Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising identifiers and applies child-directed treatment. Other identifiers, including an App Set ID, may still be processed. Google states that data handled by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

For more information, see Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, ad targeting guidance, and UMP privacy guidance.

Google ML Kit

The App uses the bundled, on-device face detection feature of Google ML Kit to detect facial contours. Face images and detection results are processed on the device and are not sent to Google servers.

According to Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, the ML Kit SDK may collect the following information for diagnostics and usage analytics:

  • Device manufacturer, model, operating-system version, and available machine-learning hardware information
  • App package name and app version
  • An installation identifier that is not intended to directly identify a person or physical device
  • Performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature versions, execution events, and error codes

Google states that this information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not shared with third parties. Camera frames and face detection results are not included in this diagnostic information.

For more information, see Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and ML Kit terms and privacy information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features or information-handling practices change. The effective date will be updated when the policy changes. Material changes may also be announced in the App or its store listing.
