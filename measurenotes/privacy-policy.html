Measure Notes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-22 · Contact: weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com

Measure Notes: Size Tracker (the “App”) helps users record and manage measurements they have taken manually by placing them on photos. The App does not automatically determine real-world dimensions from a photo.

Information Stored by the App

Photos selected or captured by the user, record names, locations, notes, measurement values, units, and measurement-line coordinates are stored in the App's private internal storage. This information is not sent to the developer's servers. The App does not provide user registration or user accounts.

When the App is uninstalled, records in its internal storage are deleted. When the user deletes a record or photo in the App, the corresponding local data is deleted. Temporary images created for sharing are stored in the device's cache and may be removed by the operating system.

Photos and Sharing

The App uses the Android system photo picker or an external camera app. The App itself does not request camera permission or broad access to the device's photo library. Selected or captured photos are copied into the App's internal storage, and the original photo's EXIF location data is not retained during that copy.

When the user chooses to share, the App sends a JPEG containing the overlaid measurements to the external app selected by the user. Processing by that external app is governed by the external app's privacy policy.

Advertising and Automatically Collected Data

The App may display banner advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following information with Google for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and security purposes:

  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • App interaction information, such as app launches and taps
  • Diagnostic information about App and advertising SDK performance
  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID in Android settings. Depending on the user's region and applicable legal requirements, the App may present consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform.

For more information about Google's processing, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13. The target age group must be confirmed before the Play Console listing is published. If the intended audience is changed to include children under 13, this policy and the App's advertising and Families policy configuration will be reviewed before distribution.

Security and Retention

Local records are stored in Android app-specific storage and are excluded from Android automatic backup. Although no method of storage can guarantee complete security, the App is designed to avoid unnecessary server transmission and broad photo-library permissions.

Retention and deletion of data processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are governed by Google's policies and the user's advertising settings. The App developer does not operate separate user accounts or a server-side user database.

Changes and Contact

If the App's features, advertising SDK, or data-handling practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date will be revised. Questions may be sent to weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com.
