LottoPocket Privacy Policy

About this policy

LottoPocket (로또포켓), package com.loganchoi.lottoKR, is provided by Logan CHOI. This policy applies to the closed-test version 1.1.0, including its Google test advertising. Initial Google Play distribution is planned for the Republic of Korea. Contact: weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com. Effective date: November 3, 2026.

Records stored on your device

Saved number combinations, labels, notes, favorites, trash, manually entered draw records and presets are stored in the app’s private device storage. Theme, generation preferences and the advertising-suppression expiry are stored locally. The app has no account, developer server, cloud synchronization, Firebase Analytics or remote crash-reporting integration of its own. It does not send number records or notes to our server or add them as advertising request parameters.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.4.0 and User Messaging Platform SDK 4.0.0 with official Google test ad IDs. Test ads and consent requests can still communicate with Google. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, ad/app interactions, and diagnostic or crash information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, device, consent and Google settings. Google is the recipient/provider; we do not operate a database of these SDK records. Google may process data outside Korea under its policies. Please see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure .

Choices and core functionality

The app checks consent information before requesting ads and provides advertising privacy options when the SDK requires them. Creating and managing numbers does not require viewing an ad. The optional rewarded-ad benefit is 24 hours without ads after the SDK reward callback; this does not guarantee that every SDK or consent network connection stops. Device advertising ID controls depend on Android and device settings.

Files, clipboard and sharing

JSON and CSV export or JSON import only runs when you select it using Android’s document picker. JSON includes saved and trashed numbers, draw records and presets; theme and ad-suppression expiry are not included. Exported files are not separately encrypted by this app. Copy and share actions pass the selected text to the clipboard or chosen app. Cloud storage or sharing providers that you select apply their own policies. We do not scan your entire file storage.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Moving a number to trash is recoverable, rather than permanent deletion. Exported files and shared copies remain in their chosen destinations until you delete them there. Android automatic backup is disabled in the manifest; manufacturer device-transfer behavior can differ. Google manages retention and deletion for data processed by its SDK under its policies. We do not promise deletion of Google-held SDK records through a local record deletion. Contact us for questions; we currently do not provide a self-service request process that deletes all SDK-held data.

Security and permissions

The app uses Android’s app sandbox and AtomicFile storage. It does not add its own file encryption or app lock. Ordinary cleartext HTTP is disabled in the application manifest. Google describes Mobile Ads SDK data as encrypted in transit with TLS; all third-party practices remain subject to their policies. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions. Final merged permissions include network and advertising-related permissions from Google SDKs.

Audience and lottery-related content

This is an adult-oriented number-record utility, not an official lottery application. It offers no lottery purchase, payment, bet, monetary prize, account balance, prediction or guarantee of winning. It is not directed to children. The intended Play audience is adults; the final content rating is determined separately through the store questionnaire.

Changes and contact

We will publish policy changes on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy or support inquiries, contact Logan CHOI at weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com. This policy describes this test build; production ad IDs, new SDKs or new features require the policy and Play Data safety declaration to be reviewed again.
