Times Tables — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Times Tables is a multiplication-tables learning app for children and their caregivers. No account or registration is required.

1. Information stored on your device

Practice results and progress, app preferences, and advertising frequency information are stored in the app's private storage to provide its features. The app does not ask for a child's name, age, birth date, photo, or other personal details. These local records are not uploaded to the developer or provided to advertising providers. The app has no cloud sync and disables Android backup of its data.

2. Children's privacy

This app is intended for children aged 6–12 and is designed to follow Google Play Families policies. The developer does not directly collect children's personal information through the app. Google AdMob may process the information described in the Advertising section, including IP addresses and device-related identifiers. The app does not request a child's name, age, location, contacts, photos, or microphone. Settings and all external links (privacy policy, store rating, sharing) open only after a simple grown-up check that a young child cannot complete by multiplying. Advertising uses child-directed and limited-ad settings, and the Android Advertising ID is not used.

3. Advertising

Times Tables uses Google AdMob. All ad requests use child-directed and limited-ad settings. The app does not request personalized advertising and does not access the Android Advertising ID. Ads never appear on the question screen or in the grown-up area.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device-related identifiers permitted by these settings. This processing supports advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Advertising data is encrypted in transit. See the Google AdMob data disclosure and Limited ads.

Practice records are not provided to AdMob. Google's processing, retention, and deletion practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear Times Tables' storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. The developer cannot access, restore, or remotely delete records stored only on your device.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning a child's information, contact the developer at weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com. If you email us, your email address and message are used only to handle your inquiry. Please do not include a child's photos or sensitive information.
