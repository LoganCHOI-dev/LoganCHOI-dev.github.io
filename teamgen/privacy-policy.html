team-gen Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Logan CHOI provides team-gen (com.loganchoi.teamgen), a roster and team-generation app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Rosters and team results

Participant names, attendance, team assignments, round history, and preferences are stored on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a server that receives your rosters or team results. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

Sharing and backup files

You can export backups and share team results as text or images using apps you choose. These files can contain participant names. You control the recipients and storage locations. Those services handle the files under their own privacy policies. Deleting team-gen does not delete files you exported or shared.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. The initial closed-test build uses Google's test ad identifiers. The Google SDKs may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No precise location permission is requested. We do not send roster names or team results as advertising event parameters. The app does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Depending on your region, the app may show privacy choices before requesting ads. When available, you can revisit these choices in the app's settings. Android device settings provide controls for advertising identifiers.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, and Google advertising technologies.

Retention and deletion

Local rosters and records remain on the device until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Shared image cache files older than 24 hours are removed when the next share image is generated. We cannot access or delete information held independently by Google or recipients of exported files. Google handles SDK data according to its policies; we do not specify or control Google's retention periods.

Audience

team-gen is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes and will show the revised effective date on this page.
