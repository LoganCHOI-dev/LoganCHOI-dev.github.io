Team Generator: Bracket Maker Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Logan CHOI provides Team Generator: Bracket Maker (com.loganchoi.teamgen), a roster, team generation, and bracket maker app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Rosters and team results

Participant names, attendance, team assignments, round history, and preferences are stored on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a server that receives your rosters or team results. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

Sharing and backup files

You can export backups and share team results as text or images using apps you choose. These files can contain participant names. Backup JSON files and shared text or images are not password-protected by this app. You control the recipients and storage locations. Those services handle the files under their own privacy policies. Deleting Team Generator: Bracket Maker does not delete files you exported or shared.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy choices. The Google SDKs may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No precise location permission is requested. We do not send roster names or team results as advertising event parameters. The app includes Firebase Analytics. Crashlytics is not included.

Depending on your region, the app may show privacy choices before requesting ads. When available, you can revisit these choices in the app's settings. Android device settings provide controls for advertising identifiers.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, and Google advertising technologies.

App usage analytics

When Analytics collection is permitted, Google Analytics for Firebase measures screen visits, participant additions, team generation and adjustments, participant counts, team counts, competition modes, match-recording actions, sharing-sheet openings, and additional group-space reward flows. It also measures app lifecycle and session events. We do not send participant names, roster contents, gathering or team names, scores, match results, shared content, backup files, or local record identifiers as Analytics event parameters. Google may process app-instance/device identifiers, app interactions, device and app information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses under its policies. This does not upload your rosters or team records. Google states that Analytics SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

On startup and while refreshing privacy choices, the app pauses Analytics collection. Advertising permission and Analytics permission are handled separately. In regions where UMP supplies consent choices, the app follows the Analytics storage choice; a denied choice disables collection. A fresh response indicating that consent is not required permits collection under this policy. If privacy information cannot be refreshed, Analytics collection remains paused. Privacy choices can be revisited in Settings when required. Temporary pauses preserve the last resolved choice; a resolved withdrawal disables collection and applies denied Analytics consent. The app does not provide a separate global Analytics switch.

Learn more: Firebase privacy and security and Google Analytics data disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Local rosters and records remain on the device until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Shared image cache files older than 24 hours are removed when the next share image is generated. Exported files remain in the locations or services you selected and must be deleted there. The app does not offer an in-app request mechanism to delete Google SDK data. Google handles that data according to its policies and service settings; uninstalling the app does not by itself delete data already processed by Google. For privacy questions, use the contact below or Google's privacy controls.
