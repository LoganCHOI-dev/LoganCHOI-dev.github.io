Pic-Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Pic-Grid is provided by Logan CHOI. This policy describes how the Android app (com.loganchoi.picgrid) handles information. Contact: weimaraner.logan.choi@gmail.com.

Photos and drawing projects

The app processes your selected reference photos on your device to provide grids, value studies, progress tracking, and exports. It uses the Android system photo picker rather than requesting access to your entire photo library. Copies of selected images, project names, paper and grid settings, and progress are stored in private app storage. Pic-Grid does not upload these photos or projects to the developer or send them to advertising SDKs.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The closed test uses Google's demonstration ad units. Test ads still use network services; this is not a claim that no device information is processed.

Google's SDKs may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent abuse, and manage privacy choices. SDK traffic is encrypted in transit. Pic-Grid does not request precise location permission. Ads appear in the project list, not over the drawing workspace or exported files. The app does not use Firebase Analytics, user accounts, or a developer-operated cloud service.

Where a privacy message or privacy-options entry is required and available, UMP presents it and the app provides access from Help and privacy. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Core photo and drawing features remain available when an ad cannot load.

For Google's practices, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Exporting and sharing

When you choose to save or share an image or PDF, the selected file is sent to the document provider or app you choose. A cloud provider you select may upload the file under its own policy. Exported images do not include location EXIF metadata. Pic-Grid does not operate a public gallery or messaging service.

Retention and deletion

Local projects remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Deleting a project removes its settings; its source copy can be retained while another project uses it, or where an unreadable project prevents safe deletion. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local data. Exported files remain at your chosen destination and must be deleted there. The app excludes project data from automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Google handles SDK data according to its policies and applicable settings; the developer cannot delete Google's records by deleting a local project. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email below. Do not send your photos unless needed for support. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations.

Audience

Pic-Grid is intended for people aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16.

Security and changes

The app uses Android private storage for local projects. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the app's practices change; the effective date will be revised.
