Privacy Policy — Teleprompter Studio

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Teleprompter Studio ("the app") is an offline teleprompter and video-recording tool. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Your scripts and recordings are stored on your device. Depending on your device settings, Android may back up scripts and settings to your device backup or transfer them to another device. The app has no account system and no server of its own. The advertising SDK may process data as described below. If an on-device speech recognizer is unavailable, the system speech-recognition service may process audio according to its provider's privacy policy.

Scripts you create

Scripts you write, paste, or import are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or sent to the advertising SDK, and are not written to logs. If Android device backup is enabled, your scripts and settings may be included in your device's cloud backup or a device-to-device transfer, depending on the device and its settings. You can manage backup in your device settings. Backup services are governed by their providers' terms and privacy policies.

Camera and video recording

If you use the camera recording feature, the app requests the Camera permission. Video is captured on your device and saved to your device's shared media storage (the Movies folder). Recordings are not uploaded anywhere by the app; you choose when to share them using your device's share sheet. The camera is used only while you are on the recording screen.

Microphone, audio recording, and voice-follow

If you record video with sound, or use the voice-follow feature, the app requests the Microphone permission.

  • Audio recording: audio is recorded together with your video and saved locally alongside it.
  • Voice-follow (speech recognition): to scroll the prompter as you speak, the app sends microphone audio to a speech-recognition service to convert speech to text. On supported devices (Android 12 and later) the app prefers the on-device recognizer, so audio is processed locally and not sent off the device. When an on-device recognizer is not available, the app uses the system speech-recognition service provided by your device, which may process audio according to that service provider's own privacy policy. The app itself does not store the recognized audio or transcribed text; it uses the text only to move the prompter position in real time.

You can use the prompter without granting the Camera or Microphone permission; the text simply scrolls at a steady speed.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may collect and process device and usage information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, interactions, and diagnostics. Where required, the app shows a consent form through UMP before personalized ads are requested. For details, see Google's policies at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543. The app does not use Firebase Analytics.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate a server, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect your name, email, scripts, or recordings.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience aged 16 and older and is not directed at children.
