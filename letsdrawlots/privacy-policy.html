This Privacy Policy explains how Let’s Draw Lots: Random Picker (the “App”), package name com.loganchoi.letsdrawlots, handles information when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following information locally on your device so that a draw can be restored after the App is closed:

  • draw result text and the number of papers for each result;
  • optional participant names entered by the user;
  • the current draw state, paper positions, draw order, and revealed-result history; and
  • local app preferences needed to operate the App.

This information is used only to provide the App’s draw, restore, and history features. The App does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server that receives this locally stored draw information. Android backup is disabled for the App.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements. It also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request consent where required and to provide available privacy choices.

According to Google’s documentation, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following categories of information:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad-related interactions;
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance and crash-related information; and
  • device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers.

Google may process this information for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The information actually processed may vary depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, the type of ads served, and Google’s services and configurations.

The App does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone for its draw features. The developer does not intentionally send the draw text, participant names, or draw history stored by the App to a developer-operated server.

For more information about Google’s practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law or Google policy, the App uses the UMP SDK to display a consent or privacy message before requesting ads. Depending on your region and the available message configuration, you may be able to choose between personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertising options.

When Google indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the App displays a Privacy Options control that lets you reopen Google’s privacy choices. You may also reset or delete the Android advertising ID through your device settings where that option is available.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored draw information remains on your device until it is overwritten through normal App use, you clear the App’s storage, or you uninstall the App.

The developer does not provide user accounts and does not keep the App’s draw information on a developer-operated server. Therefore, there is no developer-held account or server-side draw record to delete on request. Information processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google’s policies and your Google, advertising, consent, and device settings.

5. Security

The App is designed to keep draw content on the device, and Android backup is disabled. Information handled by Google’s advertising services is transmitted using encryption as described in Google’s SDK documentation. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Age eligibility

The App is intended for users aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16. The App does not provide account creation, social networking, communication, or user-to-user content-sharing features. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact the developer using the email address below.

7. International processing

Google may process information through systems located outside your country or region. Such processing is governed by Google’s applicable terms, privacy policy, and data-transfer safeguards.
