where is my car Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Developer: Logan Choi
Android package: com.loganchoi.whereismycar

where is my car helps you remember a parking place using photos and an optional map location. This policy explains local parking records and the Google services used for maps, advertising, consent and usage analytics. Earlier versions may have different advertising and analytics settings.

1. Parking information on your device

The app stores photos you take, capture times, optional approximate or precise parking coordinates and accuracy, and preferences such as retention periods, language and widget visibility in app-specific storage on your device. Photos are not added to the public gallery and location EXIF metadata is not written to them. Parking photos and records are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

We do not operate a server that receives your parking photos or saved coordinates. The app does not send photo content or parking coordinates as advertising or analytics event parameters. It has no account or login system.

2. Permissions and maps

Camera permission is used to take photos. Approximate or precise location permission is optional and used temporarily while the relevant app feature is in the foreground. The app does not request background location or continuously track your movements. Denying location permission does not prevent photo recording.

Google Maps SDK for Android and Google Play services provide maps and foreground location. Google may process IP addresses, device and request metadata, SDK identifiers, map interactions, and SDK crash metrics and stack traces, including abnormal terminations. Google uses this information to maintain and improve SDK stability and Google services. Internet access also supports the advertising, consent and analytics services described below.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob for banner ads and occasional interstitial ads following eligible parking-completion actions. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), advertising or other device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. These support ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Advertising partners may process information according to your consent choices and their policies.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable consent messages and privacy choices. Where required, you can consent, decline or manage options before eligible ad requests. If available for your region, use Privacy options in the app settings to review or withdraw your choices. Ad availability and personalisation depend on your choices, regional requirements and Google settings; declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all ads.

4. Usage analytics

Production versions use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and reliability. Collection is paused while the applicable consent state is unresolved. Where analytics consent is required, collection starts only when analytics storage is permitted; where it is not required, collection starts automatically. There is no separate usage-analytics setting in the app. When Privacy choices are available in settings, you can review or withdraw applicable consent; denying analytics storage stops future Firebase Analytics collection.

When active, analytics may process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, usage events and approximate regional information. Our custom events describe record creation, photo addition, parking-completion choices and results, app language, entry source and ad load/display outcomes. They do not contain photos, coordinates, file paths or free-form parking notes. AdMob advertising measurement is separate from Firebase Analytics collection.

5. Retention and deletion

Local parking records expire after 72 hours by default. You can choose a different retention period or no automatic deletion, explicitly keep a record, or delete it in the app. Expired records are removed when the app or its scheduled cleanup runs; device restrictions may delay cleanup. Kept records are excluded from expiry. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local app data.

Deleting local records or withdrawing analytics consent does not automatically erase information already processed by Google or advertising partners. Their retention practices depend on the service, its settings and applicable requirements. Contact us for privacy requests; we may need sufficient information to identify relevant data without collecting unnecessary parking information.

6. Your choices and rights

You can deny or revoke camera/location permissions in Android settings, change retention, delete or keep records, hide widget photos and use the available privacy options to manage applicable consent. Android also provides advertising ID controls. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

7. Service providers and security

Google and relevant advertising partners may process information on servers outside your country. Their privacy policies describe applicable safeguards and processing. Local parking data is stored in the app's private storage, and Google SDK communications use network security protections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.
